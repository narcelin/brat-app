# Brat Olympics — Rules

## How a week works

Three objectives drop each week: one **Easy**, one **Hard**, one **Unhinged**.

1. **Submit.** Do the objectives, capture proof in the app. Nobody sees your proof
   yet — everything stays hidden until submissions close.
2. **Reveal.** Submissions close and everything unlocks at once.
3. **Vote.** Rank everyone's attempts on each objective.
4. **Results.** Points are awarded, the leaderboard updates, the week closes.

## Points

| Tier | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 15 | 10 | 5 | 2 |
| Hard | 30 | 20 | 10 | 3 |
| Unhinged | 50 | 35 | 20 | 5 |

Everyone who entered and didn't medal gets effort points. Season totals carry
across all eight weeks.

## Voting

- Everyone votes on every objective, whether or not you entered it.
- You rank as many places as there are entrants. Two entrants, you rank two.
- **You cannot vote for yourself.**
- If only one person attempted an objective, it becomes a yes/no vote — did they
  actually do it? Majority of the votes cast and they take 1st.
- You only win the places that were actually contested. No medal for a race you
  ran alone.

Voting is how proof gets verified. Something that looks fake or low-effort just
doesn't get votes.

## Proof

- Photo, or video up to 60 seconds
- **Recorded in the app** — you can't upload from your camera roll
- Trim your video down to 15 seconds of the good part — that's what everyone sees
- The full recording is saved to your phone and viewable in the app

## Things that are allowed

Vote trading is allowed. If you and a friend agree to rank each other 1st every
week, that's between you and everyone who finds out.
